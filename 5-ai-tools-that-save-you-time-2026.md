# 5 AI Tools That Actually Save You Time in 2026

I test AI tools for a living. Most are overhyped. These five actually deliver.

## 1. Claude for Research
Anthropic's AI handles long documents better than anything else. Feed it a 50-page report and get a 2-paragraph summary that captures everything important.

## 2. Perplexity for Fact-Checking
Google gives you links. Perplexity gives you answers with sources. The free tier is enough for most people.

## 3. n8n for Automation
Connect your apps without code. Automate email sorting, data entry, and file organization. Free and open source.

## 4. Otter.ai for Meetings
Records and transcribes automatically. I use it for every client call. The search feature finds any topic across all recordings.

## 5. Notion AI for Organization
Built into Notion's free tier. Summarizes your notes, drafts documents, and keeps everything organized.

## Bottom Line
Start with the free versions. Only upgrade when you hit a specific limitation that costs more than the subscription.
