## What an AI Automation Agency Does

An AI automation agency builds workflows that connect business apps and automate repetitive tasks. You use no-code platforms like Make.com, Zapier, and n8n to create systems that save companies time and money.

## Getting Started: Minimum Viable Stack

1. **Make.com** (free tier) - Visual automation builder, 1,800+ app connections
2. **ChatGPT API** ($20/month) - Add AI intelligence to workflows
3. **Loom** (free) - Record demos for prospects

That is it. Total cost: $20/month.

## Finding Clients: The 4-Step Method

### 1. Pick One Niche
Real estate agents, e-commerce stores, coaches, dentists. Pick one. Go deep.

### 2. Join Their Communities
Facebook groups, Reddit, industry forums. Read for one week. Identify the most common repetitive-task complaint.

### 3. Build a Free Demo
Create a working automation that solves that exact problem. Record a 2-minute Loom walkthrough.

### 4. Offer It for Free (Once)
Find one business owner in your niche. Offer the automation at no cost in exchange for a testimonial. Document the time saved and results.

> **One real case study is worth more than a hundred cold emails.**

## The Business Models

There is more than one way to structure your agency:

- **Project-based** - One-time builds with optional support add-ons
- **Retainer** - Monthly fee for ongoing maintenance and optimization
- **Template sales** - Sell pre-built automation blueprints
- **Consulting** - Strategy and audit services for larger companies
- **White-label** - Build automations for other agencies to resell
