## 5 Digital Products You Can Create With AI Today

### 1. Printable Planners and Journals

Use Canva AI or ChatGPT with DALL-E to design daily planners, budget trackers, meal planners, and fitness journals. The productivity niche alone is a multi-billion dollar market. One well-designed planner can sell hundreds of copies on Etsy at $5 to $15 each.

### 2. Social Media Templates

Businesses need Canva templates for Instagram posts, Pinterest pins, and TikTok thumbnails. Use Canva AI to generate on-brand template sets. Package 50 templates and sell for $27. The demand never slows because platforms keep changing their formats.

### 3. AI Art and Print-on-Demand Designs

Midjourney and DALL-E produce professional artwork in seconds. Upload designs to Redbubble, Printful, or sell directly as wall art downloads. Abstract art, motivational quotes with artistic backgrounds, and niche-specific illustrations all sell well.

### 4. eBooks and Digital Guides

ChatGPT can outline and draft a 50-page guide in an afternoon. Your job: fact-check, add personal experience, format it professionally. Topics like "Meal Prep for Busy Parents" or "Beginner's Guide to Crypto" consistently sell. List on Gumroad or Amazon KDP.

### 5. Notion Templates

Notion has over 100 million users and most of them want better templates. AI helps design dashboards, project trackers, and habit builders. A good Notion template sells for $15 to $45 and the top creators make six figures annually.

## Where to Sell Your Digital Products

**Etsy** is the easiest starting point. Millions of buyers already search for digital downloads daily. The listing fee is $0.20 and Etsy takes 6.5% per sale.

**Gumroad** gives you your own storefront. No listing fees. They take 10% per sale. Better for building a brand and collecting email addresses.

**Creative Market** is higher-end. More design-savvy audience. They approve shops manually, but once in, the average order value is higher.

**Your own website** using Shopify or WooCommerce. Zero platform fees. Full control. Best once you have a catalog and an audience.

## How to Price for Profit

Beginners almost always underprice. Here is a better framework:

- **Templates and printables**: $7 to $27
- **eBook guides**: $9 to $39
- **Full template bundles**: $27 to $97
- **Courses and masterclasses**: $47 to $197

Price based on value delivered, not on how long it took you to create. A $27 template that saves someone 10 hours of work is underpriced.

## The Launch Formula That Works

1. **Create one product** -- do not build a catalog yet. Ship one thing.
2. **Optimize the listing** -- strong title, clear description, preview images showing exactly what they get
3. **List on Etsy first** -- it has built-in traffic. You do not need an audience
4. **Share on Pinterest** -- digital product pins drive consistent traffic for months
5. **Build an email list** -- Gumroad collects emails. Use them for future launches

One product that sells 5 copies per week at $15 earns you $300 per month. Ten products at that rate is $3,000. The math scales beautifully.

## The Biggest Mistakes to Avoid

- **Perfectionism**: ship at 80% done. You can improve later
- **Too many products**: master one before starting the next
- **Ignoring listings**: great SEO copy matters more than great design
- **No preview images**: buyers need to see exactly what they are purchasing
