## What Is an AI SEO Content Agency?

It's a one-person operation (you) producing 4 to 8 search-optimized articles per client per month. You use AI tools like Claude or ChatGPT for research and first drafts, then you edit for accuracy, add original insights, optimize the on-page SEO, and publish.

The result: articles that actually rank, written in a quarter of the time.

- **Small businesses are spending more on SEO.** The average US small business now budgets $1,900/month on AI-related freelance work -- up 3.4x from last year.
- **The GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) boom is just starting.** AI Overviews now appear in 68% of commercial searches. Someone needs to optimize for that. That someone could be you.

## The Stack You Need

You don't need expensive tools. Here's the 2026 starter stack:

- **Claude 3.7 Sonnet or ChatGPT** for drafting and research ($20/month)
- **Google Search Console** for tracking what's working (free)
- **Surfer SEO or Frase** for content optimization ($69-$99/month)
- **Canva** for custom images and infographics (free tier works)
- **WordPress** for publishing (free, self-hosted)

Total cost: under $120/month. One client at $2,000/month and you're profitable.

## How to Land Your First Client

The fastest path:

1. **Pick one niche.** "AI SEO agency" sells nothing. "AI SEO content for dental clinics in Texas" books calls this week.
2. **Build one sample.** Write a 2,000-word, fully optimized article for a real (or imaginary) local business. Show the keyword research, the brief, and the final piece.
3. **Post on LinkedIn.** Share the process, not just the result. "Here's how I wrote a ranking article for a Phoenix plumber using Claude + manual editing" gets attention.
4. **Offer a $500 pilot.** One article. One month. Prove the value, then upsell to a monthly retainer.
